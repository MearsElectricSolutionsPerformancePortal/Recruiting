5. Commit the changes to main. If GitHub reports no changes, these files are already there; open Actions and re-run the failed pages build and deployment job instead.
6. Once deployment succeeds, open https://mearselectricsolutionsperformanceportal.github.io/Recruiting/ and refresh.
