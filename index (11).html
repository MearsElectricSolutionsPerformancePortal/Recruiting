Construct Your Career With Mears

Laborer

Laborers work as part of a field crew completing underground utility construction. The work is hands-on, outdoors, and physically demanding, with a strong focus on safe execution and supporting the crew.

Why Join Mears?

  • Build America: Mears is a leading provider of engineering and construction solutions across critical infrastructure markets.
  • Professional growth: Mears offers training programs and career advancement pathways.
  • Competitive compensation: Mears offers wages and benefits designed to support employees and their families.
  • Community impact: Mears gives back to the communities it serves.

Safety That Brings You Home

At Mears, safety is a core priority. Employees are expected to take ownership of their own safety, follow company and client requirements, and help identify and reduce risk throughout the work.

What You'll Do

  • Load and help load materials and equipment.
  • Use hand tools to dig safely near existing underground utilities.
  • Prepare equipment before mobilizing to a job site.
  • Help secure the work site, equipment, and materials at the end of each shift.
  • Prepare job, equipment, and safety documentation as needed.
  • Perform other crew support duties as assigned.

What You'll Need

  • Valid driver's license.
  • Ability to pass required pre-employment screening.
  • Ability to stand or walk for extended periods and repeatedly lift up to 50 pounds.
  • Ability to kneel, crawl, climb, and reach as required by field work.

What Mears Offers

  • Paid technical and professional training.
  • A defined path for future growth.
  • Weekly payroll.
  • Competitive wages and benefits, including retirement benefits.
