Area Safety Manager

The Area Safety Manager provides leadership and strategic support for regional safety initiatives, ensuring compliance with company, client, and regulatory standards across multiple project sites. The role fosters a culture of safety, accountability, and continuous improvement while supporting operations throughout the assigned area.

  • Competitive compensation: Enjoy competitive wages and excellent benefits.

Safety That Brings You Home

At Mears, safety is a core priority. Employees are expected to take ownership of their own safety, follow company and client requirements, and help identify and reduce risk throughout the work.

Essential Duties

  • Lead and coordinate safety programs to support compliance with OSHA and other applicable regulations.
  • Conduct field inspections and audits, including informal worksite visits and formal documented inspections.
  • Perform root-cause analyses of incidents and ensure timely written reporting.
  • Identify and evaluate hazards associated with electrical installations, underground construction, pipeline work, and transportation activities.
  • Assess risk severity and implement controls to reduce exposure.
  • Participate in Job Hazard Analyses and recommend controls to eliminate or reduce hazards.
  • Develop and deliver safety meeting agendas and safety alerts based on field observations and audit findings.
  • Administer and support emergency response, accident prevention, and regulatory-compliance programs.
  • Collaborate with leadership, operations, and other safety professionals to align safety strategies.
  • Coach management and employees on safe work practices and proactive hazard recognition.
  • Provide guidance on occupational health, safety, and environmental requirements.
  • Ensure field personnel receive required safety training and site-specific orientation.
  • Support onboarding and orientation for new employees.
  • Oversee safety reporting, PPE management, and equipment inspections.
  • Assist with injury case management as needed.
  • Promote a strong and visible safety culture.

Key Qualifications

  • Three to five years of progressive experience in field safety or construction safety management.
  • Bachelor's degree in occupational safety and health or a related field.
  • Valid driver's license with an acceptable driving record.
  • Working knowledge of excavation and confined-space safety.
  • Strong understanding of OSHA and DOT regulations and compliance practices.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Adobe PDF.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong organization, time management, and multitasking skills.
  • Ability to influence and collaborate across teams and organizational levels.
  • Regular travel throughout the assigned area.
  • Ability to support on-call needs as assigned.
  • Ability to lift up to 50 pounds and work in variable field conditions.
  • Ability to pass required pre-employment screening.
