Apprentice Electrician

The Apprentice Electrician supports Journeyman Electricians with installation, repair, and maintenance of electrical systems while developing trade skills through formal classes and in-house training.

Your Future Starts Here.

Mears Pays for the Schooling.

4 YEARS OF TRAINING. 8,000 HOURS OF EXPERIENCE. A CAREER YOU CAN BUILD ON.

The Mears Electric Solutions Apprenticeship Program combines hands-on field experience with structured classroom instruction to help you develop the skills to become a journeyman electrician. Mears pays for your apprentice schooling in full.

8,000 hours of hands-on experience Build your skills over approximately four years, working alongside experienced journeyman electricians. Learn to install, maintain, and repair electrical systems, read blueprints, handle materials, and use electrical tools and equipment safely.
576 hours of classroom instruction Through the four-year IEC Rocky Mountain (IECRM) training program, study electrical theory, wiring methods, blueprint reading, and the National Electrical Code. That’s approximately 144 classroom hours per year, with lessons you can apply on the job.
One evening of school each week IECRM classes typically meet from 5:30–9:30 p.m., August through May, allowing you to work in the field while completing your education. Apprentices must maintain passing grades and participate in additional Mears in-house training.
A path toward your journeyman license Your field experience and classroom education help prepare you to meet licensing requirements and take the journeyman electrician exam. Along the way, you’ll build the technical knowledge, safety practices, and problem-solving skills that support a long-term career in the trade.
Bring the commitment. Build the skills. Let Mears invest in your future.

Training details above reflect the IECRM pathway in Colorado and Wyoming. School schedules and licensing requirements vary by location.

Safety That Brings You Home

At Mears, safety is a core priority. Employees are expected to take ownership of their own safety, follow company and client requirements, and help identify and reduce risk throughout the work.

Essential Duties

  • Follow company safety policies and work under an assigned Journeyman Electrician.
  • Assist with installation, repair, testing, and maintenance of wiring, equipment, fixtures, and electrical systems.
  • Support warehouse, material-handling, and prefabrication work as needed.
  • Learn to read blueprints and apply building and safety code requirements.
  • Attend weekly schooling and in-house training.
  • Work overtime or irregular hours when required.

Key Qualifications

  • Ability to work effectively as part of a team.
  • Strong attention to safety.
  • Effective oral communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong work ethic and ability to work independently when appropriate.
