Estimator

The Estimator develops bids and proposals for underground electric construction, including excavation, conduit, concrete-encased duct banks, vaults, and surface restoration. The role combines field knowledge with cost analysis, risk assessment, communication, and organization.

  • Competitive compensation: Mears offers wages and benefits designed to support employees and their families.
  • Community impact: Mears gives back to the communities it serves.

Safety That Brings You Home

At Mears, safety is a core priority. Employees are expected to take ownership of their own safety, follow company and client requirements, and help identify and reduce risk throughout the work.

Essential Duties

  • Review customer RFP packages and work requirements with operations management.
  • Research labor, equipment, material, and subcontractor cost elements.
  • Identify project risks and develop contingencies.
  • Prepare proposal clarifications, exceptions, and assumptions.
  • Present final estimates for management review and risk assessment.
  • Submit approved bids by required due dates.
  • Attend pre-bid meetings and document key discussion points.
  • Understand scope, construction methods, timelines, and customer expectations.
  • Visit job locations to assess site conditions and constructability.

Role Focus

  • Accurate and timely bid and proposal development.
  • Practical understanding of underground electric construction methods.
  • Strong communication, organization, and willingness to learn.
