Project Coordinator

The Project Coordinator supports the Project Manager with planning, project setup, controls, documentation, purchasing, scheduling, and coordination across the project team.

Why Join Mears?

  • Build America: Mears is a leading provider of engineering and construction solutions across critical infrastructure markets.
  • Professional growth: Mears offers training programs and career advancement pathways.
  • Competitive compensation: Mears offers wages and benefits designed to support employees and their families.
  • Community impact: Mears gives back to the communities it serves.

Safety That Brings You Home

At Mears, safety is a core priority. Employees are expected to take ownership of their own safety, follow company and client requirements, and help identify and reduce risk throughout the work.

Essential Duties

  • Support planning, organization, safety, cost, schedule, quality-control, equipment, and material objectives.
  • Support project controls, including planning, cost management, estimating takeoffs, progress tracking, reporting, scheduling, change management, risk management, and closeout.
  • Set up new projects and coordinate locates, subcontractors, permit requirements, and site schedules.
  • Support execution, purchasing, engineering, accounting, cost, and construction administration.
  • Maintain project time-entry and meeting documentation.
  • Maintain regular customer contact and obtain project-specific information.

Role Focus

  • Works under the guidance of the Project Manager.
  • Coordinates tasks of varying complexity across multiple project functions.
  • Helps keep project information, schedules, permits, purchasing, and reporting organized.

Benefits & Growth

  • Paid on-the-job technical and professional growth opportunities.
  • Established career path for future advancement.
  • 401(k) and Roth 401(k) retirement savings plans.
  • Health, prescription, dental, and vision plans.
  • Life and disability insurance plans.
  • Financial wellness program.
  • Employee assistance program.
  • Employee discount programs.
