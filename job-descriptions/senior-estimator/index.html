Senior Electrical Estimator

The Senior Electrical Estimator is responsible for the timely and accurate development of bids and proposals for electrical construction work. The role combines practical field knowledge, cost analysis, risk assessment, customer communication, and post-award support.

Essential Duties

  • Review customer RFP packages and project requirements with operations management.
  • Research and define cost elements including labor, equipment, materials, and subcontractors.
  • Identify project risks and develop contingencies.
  • Prepare proposal clarifications, exceptions, and assumptions.
  • Present final estimates for management review and risk assessment.
  • Submit approved bids by required due dates.
  • Attend pre-bid meetings and document key discussion points.
  • Develop a thorough understanding of scope, construction methods, timelines, and customer expectations.
  • Visit job locations to assess site conditions and constructability.
  • Provide post-award documentation and support to field operations, project management, and administrative teams.
  • Participate in contract pre-planning meetings.
  • Assist with project deliverables, change orders, and customer issue resolution.
  • Maintain estimate backup files.
  • Perform additional duties as assigned.

Key Qualifications

  • Five or more years of estimating experience in I&E for oil and gas facilities and/or manufacturing construction projects preferred.
  • Trimble Accubid experience preferred.
  • Expert proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
  • Proficient technical writing skills.
  • Ability to work independently with minimal guidance.
  • Ability to build and maintain client relationships.
  • Excellent oral and written communication skills.
  • Excellent organizational skills.
  • Ability to travel for site visits and client meetings.
  • Ability to pass required pre-employment screening.
