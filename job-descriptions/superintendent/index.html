Electrical Superintendent

The Electrical Superintendent is a leadership role responsible for managing electrical construction projects, including multiple crews and foremen. The role requires advanced planning, risk management, subcontractor coordination, close partnership with project managers, and strong ownership of productivity, quality, safety, and team development.

  • Professional growth: Mears offers training programs and career advancement pathways.
  • Competitive compensation: Mears offers wages and benefits designed to support employees and their families.
  • Community impact: Mears gives back to the communities it serves.

Essential Duties

  • Lead and manage all aspects of field operations.
  • Plan, coordinate, and execute project deliverables.
  • Oversee project layout and buy-out.
  • Attend turnover and coordination meetings, including QA/QC and commissioning.
  • Collaborate with Project Managers on forecasted hours and scheduling.
  • Order materials and tools according to project schedule forecasts.
  • Plan and manage eight-week work plans and track productivity.
  • Conduct site inspections for code, quality, and safety compliance.
  • Diagnose and troubleshoot complex electrical problems during construction.
  • Maintain accurate project documentation, including daily logs, progress reports, and material records.
  • Provide training and mentorship to foremen, electricians, and apprentices.
  • Manage subcontractors and oversee foremen with clear schedule and productivity plans.
  • Serve as primary contact for customers, subcontractors, and vendors.
  • Drive team performance in productivity, quality, and safety.
  • Recruit, mentor, and develop employees to support succession planning and career growth.
  • Lead and participate in job hazard analyses and safety briefings.
  • Manage project financials, including change orders and schedule impacts.
  • Manage planning and execution of projects and contracts.

Key Qualifications

  • Journeyman License required.
  • OSHA 30 certification.
  • Completed Foreman 3 training and demonstrated competency.
  • Competent Person Safety Training.
  • PMP training or certification preferred.
  • Leadership training and advanced risk-management knowledge.
  • Proficiency in Excel, Bluebeam, and scheduling tools, including P6.
  • Ability to manage crews of up to 100 and full projects independently.
  • Seven or more years of electrical field experience with leadership responsibilities.
  • Valid driver's license.
  • DOT Medical Card if driving a company vehicle.
  • Excellent computer, oral, and written communication skills.
  • Ability to pass required pre-employment screening.

Physical Activity & Work Environment

  • Travel as needed.
  • Exposure to hot and cold weather.
  • Walking on uneven and variable terrain for extended periods.
  • Sitting for extended periods in an office environment.
  • Frequent standing, walking, stooping, climbing, bending, and reaching.
  • Occasional lifting of objects weighing up to 50 pounds.
