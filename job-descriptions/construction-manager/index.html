Construction Manager

The Construction Manager leads on-site construction activities and coordinates crews, schedules, materials, and compliance requirements to deliver projects safely, on time, and within scope. The role connects field operations with project management while driving quality, performance, and team development.

Essential Duties

  • Organize and manage construction activities on a job site.
  • Coordinate scheduling and communication.
  • Act as liaison to project management regarding bids, subcontracting, progress, and delays.
  • Support project procedures, schedules, cost controls, and reporting.
  • Develop and coach employee performance.
  • Prepare and review plans, specifications, drawings, and schedules.
  • Review and approve time and expenses for field crews.
  • Provide day-to-day direction to field activities, including manpower levels, employee relations, workmanship, materials conformity, and design compliance.
  • Coordinate movement of tools, materials, and equipment.
  • Identify potential scope changes and advise the team of impacts.
  • Maintain a daily job-site log.
  • Coordinate construction services and activities.
  • Conduct inspections at critical phases.
  • Maintain a safe, secure, and healthy work environment.

Key Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in a related field preferred.
  • Five or more years of construction management experience.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Outlook, Word, and Excel.
  • Excellent interpersonal, communication, and presentation skills.
  • Current knowledge of regulations, industry trends, and technologies.
  • Strong organization, time management, and prioritization skills.
  • Ability to work in variable weather and on variable terrain.
  • Ability to lift up to 50 pounds.
  • Ability to pass required pre-employment screening.
