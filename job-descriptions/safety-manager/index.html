Field Safety Manager

The Field Safety Manager leads on-site safety efforts through inspections, training, compliance oversight, communication, and coaching. The role helps build a strong safety culture while supporting field teams and project leadership.

Safety That Brings You Home

At Mears, safety is a core priority. Employees are expected to take ownership of their own safety, follow company and client requirements, and help identify and reduce risk throughout the work.

Essential Duties

  • Monitor and coordinate company, regulatory, and customer safety and health programs.
  • Lead on-site safety efforts and ensure compliance with company, client, and regulatory standards.
  • Conduct safety inspections, job-site audits, and incident investigations.
  • Support continuous improvement and alignment with company goals and values.
  • Advise and train personnel on safety topics, policies, and procedures.
  • Coach employees on hazardous conditions, safe behaviors, and corrective actions.
  • Deliver new-hire orientation and ongoing safety training.
  • Participate in client and internal project meetings and represent safety priorities.
  • Manage safety documentation, including reports, training records, and incident logs.
  • Respond to emergency situations and conduct incident investigations.

Key Qualifications

  • Two or more years of experience in a relevant safety role.
  • One or more years of leadership experience.
  • Pipeline experience preferred.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office.
  • Experience conducting safety training.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Strong prioritization, organization, adaptability, and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to travel and support on-call needs as assigned.
  • Ability to pass required pre-employment screening.
