Laborer

Laborers are an integral part of the Mears field team, working alongside experienced crews to safely complete underground utility construction projects. This is a hands-on role for people who enjoy working outdoors and being part of the work that builds and maintains critical infrastructure.

What You'll Do

  • Load and assist with loading materials and equipment.
  • Use hand tools to dig safely near existing underground utilities.
  • Prepare equipment before mobilizing to job sites.
  • Help secure the work site, equipment, and materials at the end of each shift.
  • Prepare documents, reports, and job aids related to work, equipment, and safety.
  • Support the crew with other duties needed to complete projects safely and effectively.

What You'll Need

  • A valid driver's license.
  • Ability to successfully complete required pre-employment screening.
  • Ability to work outdoors and perform physically demanding construction work.
  • Ability to stand or walk for extended periods and perform repeated lifting of up to 50 pounds.
  • Ability to kneel, crawl, climb, and reach as required by field work.
  • Ability to follow safety requirements and work effectively as part of a crew.

What Mears Offers

  • Paid on-the-job technical and professional training.
  • A defined career path with opportunities for future growth.
  • Weekly payroll.
  • Competitive wages.
  • Industry-leading benefits, including retirement benefits.
